# Briarwatch — game description

## Identity

You are a roadwarden in the Briar March, a rural frontier whose small settlements
survive through trade and mutual obligation. The protection of an old crown has
receded. Roads that once joined the villages now belong to hungry packs and
organized raiders. Briarwatch remains a place of shelter, repair and human company.

This is an original medieval fantasy setting. Its inspiration is the readable
perspective, deliberate combat, dangerous excursions, equipment progression and
town-to-wilderness rhythm of traditional action RPGs. It does not reuse another
game's characters, dialogue, geography, assets, classes or storyline.

## First playable experience

The player begins in a small timber-and-stone village with a sword, shield, padded
coat, three healing tonics and twenty crowns. A single connected region surrounds
it: meadow, sparse pinewood, branching paths, a charcoal camp, an eastroad camp,
an old watchtower and scattered ruins. The map shows authored roads and hostile
encounter landmarks. There are no loading transitions within the region.

The intended loop is: take Elric's objective, choose a road, learn enemy attack
timings, collect crowns and equipment, return for healing or upgrades, and clear
the tower. Delivering that news to Elric earns fifty crowns and a better sword.
Optional encounters and repeatable rest support further combat and gear testing.

The warrior's only combat action is a basic sword attack. The shield contributes
passive armor; there is no active block, dodge button, class selection or skill
tree. Movement is itself defensive: step out of a committed melee swing or away
from an arrow. Gear changes damage and flat armor. Tonics restore forty-five HP.

## Opposition

- **Road raiders** advance into close range and commit to slow, readable swings.
- **Briar bowmen** retreat when crowded, seek line of sight and fire visible arrows.
- **Greyfang wolves** close faster, circle and withdraw between short bites.

Twenty-four enemies occupy eight encounter groups. Camps mix melee and ranged
roles. The watchtower has a tougher placed raider using instance overrides, which
demonstrates stronger encounters without inventing another enemy implementation.
Enemies return home and recover when pulled beyond their leash.

## Town roles

**Mara**, smith and supplier: buy upgrades and tonics; sell unequipped finds.
**Sister Iona**, healer: restore health freely, or rest to repopulate encounters.
**Warden Elric**, roadkeeper: give the objective and its one-time reward.

## Presentation and future direction

The milestone uses original low-poly geometry, earthy greens, muted iron, warm
cloth and readable silhouettes. An orthographic camera preserves the ARPG view.
Small synthesized attack/impact sounds and combat telegraphs provide feedback.
This is foundation art, not a finished commercial asset library.

Future regions should develop the identity through abandoned fortifications,
local factions, old duties and regional threats. Expand content through authored
resources and composed scenes. The immediate next design work is combat feel and
enemy encounter tuning, followed by richer animation and environment assets.
Large skill trees, randomized affixes, bosses, crafting and procedural dungeons
are future work; none is implied to exist in this milestone.
